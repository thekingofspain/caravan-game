# Caravan

Two-player card game (Fallout: New Vegas rules). Each player builds three caravans; first to out-bid the opponent on two of three lanes wins.

## Language

### Lanes and scoring

**Caravan**:
One player's pile in one lane (three per player).
_Avoid_: track, column

**Lane**:
One of three 1:1 mini-games (C1–C3; code 0–2): AiCx vs HumanCx. Undecided on tie, big game continues.
_Avoid_: column, track, mini-game, battle

**Points**:
Sum of a caravan's number cards, with Kings doubling (5+K = 10, +K = 20).
_Avoid_: total, score, bid, value

**Sellable**:
A caravan with 21–26 points.
_Avoid_: sold (alone)

**Sold**:
A sellable caravan that also out-points the opposing caravan in its lane.
_Avoid_: winning, seller (as adjective)

**Seller**:
Winner of a lane's mini-game, if any; none on tie or empty.
_Avoid_: winner (of a lane)
**Winner**:
First to hold two or more sold lanes.
_Avoid_: seller (of the game), victor

**Busted**:
A caravan with over 26 points.
_Avoid_: over, burst

**Unsellable**:
A caravan with under 21 points (including empty).
_Avoid_: unplayable

### Cards

**Value card**:
A suited A, 2–10 (Ace counts as 1); the only cards that score points and start caravans.
_Avoid_: number card, points card

**Operation card**:
J, Q, K, or Joker as played onto a row (max three per row); never scores points. Player docs may say face card / Joker.
_Avoid_: picture card

**Row**:
One value card plus its attached face cards/Jokers (length 1–4).
_Avoid_: stack, pile, slot

**Direction**:
A caravan's ascending or descending sequence, set by its last two value cards; a suit match overrules it and resets it.
_Avoid_: sequence, order

**Caravan suit**:
The suit imposed by the last Queen (or the last row's suit); a value card matching it is always playable.
_Avoid_: trump, imposed suit

### Moves

**Opening round**:
The first three turns per player; value cards into empty caravans only, no draw, no discard, no disband.
_Avoid_: setup phase, mulligan (the reshuffle on <3 value cards is part of it)

**Play**:
Put a hand card onto a caravan (value card onto own, operation card onto either side), then draw from the shoe.
_Avoid_: attach (that's the mechanism, not the move)

**Discard**:
Drop a hand card face-up onto your own discard pile, then draw. The only face-up discard.
_Avoid_: remove, scrap

**Disband**:
Clear all rows from one of your own caravans to the proper discard piles, unseen.
_Avoid_: clear, reset, remove

**Remove**:
A Jack (its target row) or Joker (matching rows elsewhere, zero or more) effect; each removed card goes to its own deck's discard pile, unseen.
_Avoid_: discard, kill, destroy

**Shoe**:
Your undealt deck; drawn after each play or discard while cards remain.
_Avoid_: deck (in play), stock, draw pile

**Discard pile**:
Per-deck grave for spent cards; removals and disbands land unseen, manual discards land face-up.
_Avoid_: graveyard, scrap heap, shared discard
